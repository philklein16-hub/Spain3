Chocolatería San Ginés
~€12/couple
Pasadizo de San Ginés 5 · 5-min walk from JW Marriott
Daily · open 24 hours
Madrid's most iconic café, open since 1894 — and the first thing you should do after arriving. Order the porras (the larger, fluffier version of churros) with thick dark hot chocolate — dip, let the chocolate coat, eat immediately. The hot chocolate is intentionally thick, almost pudding-like — this is how it has been served in Spain since the 1800s, when it was eaten as a meal.
Sarah Porras & chocolate are vegetarian. No restrictions to worry about.
Avoid the overflow seating — when staff guide you to the secondary space, ask for the original marbled interior with green velvet seats instead. ~€6 for a plate of churros and a mug of chocolate.

Granada

July 30 – August 1 · Alternate options
Centro · Albaicín
Granada Tapas Tradition Unlike Madrid or Seville, Granada still offers FREE tapas with every drink ordered — a tradition that survives from when Alfonso X decreed wine must be served with food to prevent intoxication. Order a caña (beer) or glass of wine and a tapa arrives.

Sevilla Tapas Key Pringá (slow-cooked meat ragout on bread), salmorejo (chilled tomato cream), solomillo al whisky (pork in whisky-garlic sauce), espinacas con garbanzos (spinach with chickpeas — the Sevilla vegetarian signature), and cola de toro (bull-tail stew) are the five canonical Sevilla tapas.
